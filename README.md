Glad to see you here! I'm Vinit Shahdeo, a **Software Engineer** with a strong passion for **JavaScript** and **open-source**. I'm honored to be recognized thrice as a [GitHub Star](https://stars.github.com/profiles/vinitshahdeo/) from India! With a knack for building **event-driven architecture**, I balance code with **badminton** 🏸 and find peace in a sip of **tea** 🍵 and **poetry** 🎙️. 

Currently, I’m leading engineering efforts at an AI startup ([zzazz](https://zzazz.com/)), building scalable microservices and AI-powered platforms to turn online content into valuable, tradable assets. Before this, I built core banking platform at [Novo](https://www.novo.co/) as a Senior Backend Engineer, and earlier spent 4.5 wonderful years at [Postman](https://www.postman.com/). My notable contributions at Postman include building [Interceptor](https://learning.postman.com/docs/sending-requests/capturing-request-data/interceptor/) and [Integrations](https://www.postman.com/product/integrations/). Discover more about my [Postman journey](https://vinitshahdeo.dev/saying-goodbye-to-postman) and [valuable lessons](https://vinitshahdeo.dev/10-lessons-learned-as-software-engineer-at-postman). I've had the privilege of mentoring in [Google Summer of Code](https://blog.postman.com/join-postman-at-google-summer-of-code-2023/) (**GSoC**) at [AsyncAPI](https://github.com/asyncapi) and [Postman](https://blog.postman.com/postmans-projects-and-contributors-google-summer-of-code-2023/). You can find me at [`@vinitshahdeo-novo`](https://github.com/vinitshahdeo-novo) for my work in Novo.
